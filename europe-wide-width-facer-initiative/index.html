Do I need a complete specification?+

No. Submit the information you have. A short application need is enough to start, and our team can collect missing details during follow-up.

How are sample and trial-roll formats coordinated?+

For qualified projects, GRECHO can review an appropriate format based on the material direction, project scope and current availability. Commercial terms, freight and special preparation are confirmed for the specific project.

What is the current project stage?+

The current focus is requirements and technical evaluation. This is not an announcement of a completed production line or a confirmed commercial launch.

Does submission guarantee a November 2026 meeting?+

No. November 2026 is the planned discussion window. Each visit still requires project review, scheduling and written confirmation.
