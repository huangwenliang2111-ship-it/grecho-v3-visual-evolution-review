Document availability and review

  • Public Resource

    General product and application information for early review may be available to view directly.

  • Form Submission

    Selected Color TDS or marketing documents may be provided after a completed request where applicable.

  • Internal Review

    Full TDS, fire test documents, compliance files and SDS/MSDS may require GRECHO team review before sharing. Full TDS review considers the application, review stage and sharing scope.

  • Review Required

    Documents are checked before response against the sample, standard, market and project scope. Fire reports are matched to samples, standards and markets; compliance files to scope and validity; safety data to the applicable product and document version.

How a document request is reviewed

  • 01Share your application and document need

    Identify your board type, facer direction, target market, review stage and the documents you need.

  • 02Review the material and document scope

    GRECHO considers the application, sample status and any relevant standards or review requirements.

  • 03Confirm available documents or next steps

    Where applicable, GRECHO can confirm which documents are available or suggest what to prepare next.
